About the job
Airteam is a Berlin-based SaaS startup with an AI-powered cloud platform for roofers and solar planners. As a mid-level Computer Vision Engineer, you'll build 2D-to-3D object detection and automatic geometry reconstruction from drone data. Your work helps roofers and solar planners make informed decisions and drives a climate-neutral product.
Tasks

Develop and implement computer vision algorithms for 2D-to-3D object detection using drone data

Integrate ML models into our platform together with the engineering teams

Build end-to-end ML pipelines with GCP, Python, Argo, PyTorch and MLflow

Implement data preprocessing, model training, deployment and monitoring

Visualize and communicate findings to stakeholders



Requirements

2-4 years of commercial experience in data science/ML, including Computer Vision and Deep Learning

Experience across the full ML lifecycle (preprocessing, training, evaluation, deployment, monitoring), incl. conda and MLflow or comparable tools

Solid software engineering skills: Python, Git/GitHub, Docker/Kubernetes, cloud platforms

Strong problem-solving, communication and teamwork skills, plus a willingness to learn new tools quickly

Fluent English (written and spoken), as our team works internationally (German is a plus, but not required)



Nice-to-have

Experience in construction or a related industry

Experience with our stack: GCP, Vertex AI, PyTorch, Conda, MLflow, GitHub, Argo Workflows

Experience building and maintaining ML pipelines

A goal-oriented, "just do it" mentality
